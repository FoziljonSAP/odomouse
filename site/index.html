Your week on one card

Odomouse draws your week on one card: how far the cursor went, keys, clicks and your fastest typing. Save it as a picture or paste it straight into a chat.

It counts keys. It does not read what you type.

Odomouse notes which key was pressed so it can count it and colour the heatmap. It never stores text, passwords or screenshots.

There is no account, no advertising and nothing is sent anywhere: all the numbers sit in one file on your computer, and you can export or delete them in Settings.

The code is open under the GPL-3.0 licence, so anyone can check this.
